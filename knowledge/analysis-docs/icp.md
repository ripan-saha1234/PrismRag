# Web Prism Dynamics LLP — Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

Use this document to score how closely a prospect matches our ideal customer.

## Ideal Customer Summary

Our best-fit customers are businesses or teams that need custom software, AI/RAG solutions, or product engineering support with a clear problem, decision involvement, and realistic timeline.

## ICP Scoring Parameters (0–100)

### 1. Role & Decision Power (weight: 20%)
- **High (16–20):** Founder, CEO, CTO, VP Engineering, Product Head, Business Owner
- **Medium (8–15):** Product Manager, Engineering Manager, Operations Lead
- **Low (0–7):** Student, researcher only browsing, no buying authority

### 2. Problem Clarity (weight: 20%)
- **High:** Describes a specific business problem, use case, or product need
- **Medium:** General interest in AI, web, or mobile development
- **Low:** Vague curiosity with no project context

### 3. Service Fit (weight: 20%)
Strong fit services:
- Web / full-stack development
- Mobile apps
- AI chatbots & RAG systems
- Workflow automation
- Cloud & DevOps
- Ongoing maintenance / dedicated team

### 4. Budget & Commercial Intent (weight: 15%)
- **High:** Mentions budget, pricing tiers, enterprise plan, paid project, timeline to buy
- **Medium:** Asks about pricing or project cost without commitment
- **Low:** Only free resources, no commercial signals

### 5. AI / RAG Maturity (weight: 10%)
- **High:** Building or integrating AI, mentions documents, knowledge base, chatbot, agents
- **Medium:** Exploring AI possibilities
- **Low:** No AI relevance

### 6. Urgency & Timeline (weight: 10%)
- **High:** Launch in weeks, urgent need, active project
- **Medium:** 1–3 month horizon
- **Low:** No timeline, exploratory only

### 7. Engagement Quality (weight: 5%)
- **High:** Multiple meaningful messages, follow-up questions, detailed requirements
- **Medium:** Short but relevant questions
- **Low:** One-off generic message

## Lead Type Mapping

| ICP Score | Lead Type | Recommended Action |
|-----------|-----------|-------------------|
| 80–100 | Hot | Contact sales immediately, offer consultation |
| 55–79 | Warm | Nurture with case studies, propose discovery call |
| 0–54 | Cold | Share resources, keep in nurture flow |

## Sentiment Guidelines

- **Positive:** Interested, appreciative, enthusiastic, ready to proceed
- **Neutral:** Information gathering, factual questions, early exploration
- **Negative:** Frustrated, dissatisfied, complaining, rejecting offerings

## Disqualifiers (reduce score)

- Only wants free work with no budget signals
- Completely unrelated to software/AI services
- Spam, abuse, or off-topic trolling
- No identifiable business or project need after multiple messages

## Industries We Serve Well

Healthcare, e-commerce, education, fintech, SaaS startups, and businesses digitizing operations with AI.
